## symptoms | Symptoms
The outline does not line up with the fill, gaps show at the edges, and the design shifts to one side.

## causes | Causes
- Fabric not hooped tightly
- No stabilizer (backing) used
- Loose hoop clamps
- Too little pull compensation in the design file

## steps | Fix steps
1. Stop the machine and switch the power off.
2. Re-hoop the fabric. It should be tight like a drum, but not stretched.
3. Place a stabilizer (backing) suited to the fabric underneath.
4. Check the hoop frame clamps are firmly locked.
5. If gaps remain, ask the designer about pull compensation and underlay in the design file.
6. Run a test stitch on the same fabric.

## safety | Safety
Switch the machine off while fitting or removing the hoop; keep hands away from the moving frame.
