## symptoms | Symptoms
The needle breaks while the machine is running, sometimes with a loud click.

## causes | Causes
- Needle loose or bent
- Hoop or frame hitting the needle
- Thin needle used on thick fabric
- Design running outside the hoop

## steps | Fix steps
1. Stop the machine and switch the power off.
2. Carefully remove every piece of the broken needle. Check the bobbin area for pieces too.
3. Push a new needle fully up with the eye facing the front, then tighten the screw.
4. Check the hoop is locked onto the frame and the design fits inside the hoop.
5. Use a needle suited to the fabric thickness.
6. Test at low speed. If the needle breaks again, call a technician.

## safety | Safety
Broken needle pieces are sharp. Switch off the power and remove them with tweezers; wear glasses if needed.
