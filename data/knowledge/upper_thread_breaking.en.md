## symptoms | Symptoms
The upper thread breaks again and again while stitching. Thread ends look frayed.

## causes | Causes
- Thread path not threaded correctly
- Upper tension too tight
- Bent or blunt needle
- Poor-quality thread

## steps | Fix steps
1. Stop the machine and switch the power off.
2. Remove the upper thread and re-thread the full thread path. Make sure the thread sits between the tension discs and in the take-up lever.
3. Loosen the upper tension knob slightly.
4. Remove the old needle and fit a new one. Push it fully up and tighten the screw.
5. Use good-quality embroidery thread. The cone should turn freely on the stand.
6. Run a test stitch on scrap fabric first.

## safety | Safety
Always switch the machine off before working near the needle, thread or bobbin.
