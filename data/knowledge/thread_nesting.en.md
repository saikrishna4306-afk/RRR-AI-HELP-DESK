## symptoms | Symptoms
Thread bunches up in a tangled nest under the fabric. The machine may stop or the fabric may jam.

## causes | Causes
- Upper thread not seated in the tension discs
- Upper tension far too loose
- Bobbin inserted the wrong way
- Lint and thread bits in the bobbin area

## steps | Fix steps
1. Stop the machine and switch the power off.
2. Carefully cut the tangled thread away with scissors. Do not pull the fabric hard.
3. Re-thread the upper thread; it must sit properly between the tension discs.
4. If the upper tension is very loose, tighten it slightly.
5. Clean the bobbin area with a brush and re-insert the bobbin the right way round.
6. Run a test stitch on scrap fabric.

## safety | Safety
Switch off the power before working near the bobbin area; handle scissors carefully.
